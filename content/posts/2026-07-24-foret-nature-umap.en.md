Forests with a high degree of naturalness account for **23% of Wallonia's forests** yet only **3,3%** currently benefit from strong legal protection.

To help raise awareness, **Forêt & Naturalité** and **WWF Belgium** have created an interactive map that is freely accessible to everyone.

More than just a map, this tool also supports policymakers by highlighting the forest areas where conservation and restoration efforts should be prioritized.

For this project, **Forêt & Nature** and **WWF Belgium** chose **uMap**, an open-source tool that makes it easy to create custom interactive maps using #**OpenStreetMap** as the basemap.

https://umap.openstreetmap.fr/en/map/foret-naturalite-walnat-1_1381235
